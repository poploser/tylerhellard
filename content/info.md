#### About me. 
I’m Tyler Hellard. I’m usually a professional [copywriter](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tylerhellard/), but sometimes I write [other things](/writing/), too. One time I wrote a [novel](/terrypunchout/) that (incredibly!) became a Canadian best seller (impossibly!) eight years after it came out.

#### About Pop Loser. 
Pop Loser is a [blog](https://tylerhellard.com/poploser/) (and, for a while, newsletter) I've written on and off since 2007. It's changed a lot over the years and I've burned it all to the ground five or six times. The most recent [iteration](/poploser/) is a bit esoteric, with a little writing and a lot of links, and includes mixtapes. When each volume finishes, posts are collected on an omnibus page: Vol. [1](/poploser/vol1/), [2](/poploser/vol2/), [3](/poploser/vol3/), [4](/poploser/vol4/), [5](/poploser/vol5/), [6](/poploser/vol6/). Most previous versions of Pop Loser are archived in the following places: Newsletters #1–78 (unavailable), [#79–97](https://web.archive.org/web/20220312222936/https://us2.campaign-archive.com/home/?u=73477be0867f67a19f1fec465&id=f5d72195cb) and [#98–113](https://web.archive.org/web/20200809173730/https://www.getrevue.co/profile/poploser/#archive) (2014–2018). [Original blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20240000000000*/http://poploser.org/) ([2007](https://web.archive.org/web/20070528205939/http://www.spincity.org/blog/)–[2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140106063005/http://poploser.org/)) and [Tumblr](https://poploser.tumblr.com/) clone ([2010–2014](https://poploser.tumblr.com/archive)). From 2019 to 2024, I probably had better things to do.

#### Tech stuff.
As of July 2026, this website is powered by [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) using a custom theme and hosted at [GitHub](https://github.com/poploser/tylerhellard.com). The type is set almost entirely in [Monda](https://github.com/vernnobile/mondaFont) with icons and skulls and such from [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/). Basic stats are collected with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) (most options turned off — I'm only getting total pageviews and some limited referral data). All content is written and edited in markdown with [IA Writer](https://ia.net/writer). 

<span style="color: #fff; background: #FE5C00; padding: 0 5px;">**RSS**</span>: 🚒 [Pop Loser](https://tylerhellard.com/feed.xml) and other posts. / 🎧 [Pod Loser](	https://media.rss.com/podloser/feed.xml) for just the mixtape podcast. 

(A note on [toots](https://mstdn.ca/@poploser): Toots are not included in the RSS feed because that would be weird. They are included in the homepage firehose, but only days after the fact when I push an update. They also crosspost to [BlueSky](https://bsky.app/profile/tylerhellard.com), if that's your kink.)
