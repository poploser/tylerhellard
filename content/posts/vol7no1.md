#### The last redemption of geist.
*Vol. 7, No. 1*

Volume 6 kinda stunk. I apologize for that. I was tired coming out of Canada Reads, I decided mid-volume to port the site, I metablogged and said dull things for the sake of filling space, I got too enthusiastic about places like Bubbles, and didn’t do nearly enough mixtapes. Sure, the links are always nice and my pun game remained *on fucking point*, but everything else should be a little interesting or at least marginally amusing. Like every LLM keeps saying to me when I rage in all caps at their childlike stupidity: you deserve better. 

Welcome to **Pop Loser Volume 7: The Last Redemption of Geist**. / ✌️
